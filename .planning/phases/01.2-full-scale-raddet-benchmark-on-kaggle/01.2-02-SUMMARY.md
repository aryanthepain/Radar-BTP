## Key Changes
1. **6-Bin Multi-SNR Quantitative Evaluation Loop**:
   - Discrete SNR points: `SNR_BINS = [-20, -12, -4, 4, 12, 20]` (dB).
   - Dual-Strategy Test Frame Binning: Regex token search on filenames with fallback to 6 equal sequential chunks.
   - Computes $m\text{AP}_{50}$ and $m\text{AP}_{50:95}$ across all 6 discrete points and calculates the grand mean.
2. **Table III Exporter**:
   - Generates both `WORKING_DIR / "raddet_s128_table_iii.md"` and `WORKING_DIR / "raddet_s128_table_iii.csv"`.
   - Columns: `Model`, `Size`, `#Param (M)`, `RadDet-9T mAP50 (%)`, `RadDet-9T mAP50:95 (%)`, `FPSavg`, `Target Baseline Reference`.
   - Benchmarks paper SOTA YOLOv9-M (25.93% mAP50, 896.6 FPS) alongside reproduced YOLOv9-C and YOLOv8-M baseline.
3. **Multi-SNR Performance Curves (Fig. 3(c))**:
   - Exports `multi_snr_map_curves.png` rendering detection precision curves vs SNR dB.
   - Dark Cyber-Radar styling with neon cyan (`#00FFCC`) and neon lime (`#76FF03`) traces.
4. **Dedicated Single-Frame Latency Benchmark ($FPS_{avg}$)**:
   - Evaluates at batch size 1 to measure true cognitive EW digitizer latency without batching bias.
   - High-precision CUDA event timers (`torch.cuda.Event(enable_timing=True)`) over 100 warmup and 1,000 timed frames, synchronizing device before timing.
   - Reports mean, median, P95 latency (ms) and operational $FPS_{avg} = 1000.0 / \text{mean\_latency}$.
   - Automatically re-injects measured $FPS_{avg}$ into Table III artifacts.
5. **Multi-SNR Qualitative Detection Grid (4 Panels)**:
   - Exports `multi_snr_qualitative_grid.png` across 4 operational scenarios: High SNR (+20 dB), Moderate SNR (+4 dB), Degraded Low SNR (-20 dB), and Overlapping Complex Signals.
