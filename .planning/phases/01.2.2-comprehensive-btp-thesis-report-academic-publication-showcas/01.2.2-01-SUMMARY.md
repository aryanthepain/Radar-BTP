## Key Deliverables & Changes

### 1. Literature Breakdown Across 5 Landmark Works
- **RadDet (ICASSP 2025)**: Evaluated 40,000-frame 500 MHz wideband radar dataset, 11 signal classes, 6 SNR tiers down to -20 dB, max-hold spectrogram tiers (S-128, M-256, L-512), YOLOv9-M (1.12 ms vs 2.23 ms; 25.93% vs 47.37% mAP50 on dense frames). Identified core limitation of static uniform resolution.
- **RAVEN (CVPR 2026)**: 3D RAD tensor attention mechanism, >150 GFLOPs edge computational bottleneck.
- **AdaRadar (CVPR 2026)**: Agile chirp rate streaming requiring agile hardware PLLs; lacks perception-level adaptive compute.
- **Alexander Karlsson PhD Thesis (Saab/KTH 2023)**: Saab SIRS 1600 radar parameters ($B=160\text{ MHz}$, $T_c=58.8\ \mu\text{s}$), stepped-frequency pulse compression CNN, 2-stage TBD CNN, micro-Doppler drone classification with autoencoder QDA (96.4%), low-angle sea multipath monopulse tracking.
- **Dr. Tapas Kumar Pal DRDO Survey (IEEE SPACE 2025)**: Radar range equation with system losses, GAN clutter generation, RL-DQN beam steering, 8-bit quantization formula, and gradient saliency maps.

### 3. Mathematical Formulation of 5 Candidate Methodologies
- **Method 1 (Primary)**: Cascaded Multi-Resolution Input Triage ($128^2 \to 256^2 \to 512^2$) with entropy/prediction confidence gating ($\mathcal{H}(y) < \tau_{\text{conf}}$).
- **Method 2 (Primary)**: Cross-Resolution Knowledge Distillation transferring high-frequency representation from $512^2$ teacher to $128^2$ student ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{KD}} = (1-\alpha)\mathcal{L}_{\text{det}} + \alpha \tau^2 \mathcal{L}_{\text{KL}}$).
- **Method 3 (Primary)**: Dynamic Layer Early-Exit with internal classifier heads at intermediate backbone stages.
- **Method 4 (Exploratory)**: Self-Supervised RF Masked Autoencoder Pretraining on raw I/Q chirps.
- **Method 5 (Exploratory)**: Reinforcement Learning Policy Network for dynamic budget-constrained radar resource allocation.

### 4. Dual Mathematical Optimization Formulations
- **Constrained Optimization**:
  $$\min_{\pi} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}} [C(x, \pi(x))] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{mAP}(\pi) \ge \text{mAP}_{\text{oracle}} - \epsilon, \quad P_{95}(\text{Latency}(\pi)) \le \tau_{\text{max}}$$
- **Multi-Objective Pareto Loss**:
  $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{Pareto}}(\theta) = \mathcal{L}_{\text{det}}(\theta) + \lambda_1 \left( \frac{\text{FLOPs}(\theta)}{\text{FLOPs}_{\text{baseline}}} \right)^\gamma + \lambda_2 \max(0, \text{Latency}(\theta) - \tau_{\text{budget}})^2$$
