### 2. Central Research Thesis & Adaptive Computation Formulation
- **D-02:** Overarching Research Question:
  > *"Can we use significantly less computation for radar signal detection without sacrificing detection accuracy?"*
- **D-03:** Non-Dogmatic Exploratory Framing for BTP-1:
  - The BTP-1 report establishes the overarching thesis direction and trade-off landscape rather than prematurely locking a single rigid architecture.
  - Formulate the 5 candidate adaptive computation methodologies to be researched and benchmarked over the 3–6 month BTP-2 timeline:
    1. *Cascaded Multi-Resolution Triage*: Lightweight 128² fast-path detector handling ~80% of clear/high-SNR frames in ~1.1 ms; ambiguous, low-SNR (<0 dB), or dense overlapping frames escalated to 256² or 512² high-fidelity detectors via uncertainty gating.
    2. *Cross-Resolution Knowledge Distillation*: High-resolution 512² teacher model transferring rich multi-scale spatial-frequency representations into an ultra-compact 128² student model.
    3. *Dynamic Layer Early-Exit & Conditional Computation*: Multi-head early-exit classifiers embedded across intermediate backbone layers (e.g. GELAN / PGI blocks) that terminate inference early when prediction entropy is sufficiently low.
    4. *Self-Supervised RF Pretraining & Representation Learning*: Masked spectrogram/chirp modeling to enhance weak signal representation without requiring massive labeled frames.
    5. *Reinforcement Learning / Learned Policy Allocation*: Formulating resource allocation as a sequential Markov Decision Process (MDP) trading off accuracy against computational budget. — **Reversibility:** reversible
- **D-04:** Mathematical Formulation:
  - Present the primary **Constrained Optimization Formulation** as the central candidate in the report:
    $$\min_{\theta, \pi} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}} [C(x, \pi(x))]$$
    $$\text{subject to } \text{mAP}(f(x, \pi(x))) \ge \text{mAP}_{\text{oracle}} - \epsilon \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Latency}_{P95} \le \tau_{\max}$$
  - Also present **Multi-Objective Pareto Loss Functions** ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{det}} + \lambda \frac{C(x)}{C_{\max}} + \gamma \mathcal{U}(x)$) as an alternative exploratory formulation to be researched during BTP-2. — **Reversibility:** reversible

  - *Operational Defense & Automotive Utilities*: All-weather radar robustness (rain/fog penetration) and low-RCS electronic warfare resilience. — **Reversibility:** reversible
