## 2. Multi-SNR Stress Test Performance (Robustness Curve)

Models were subjected to synthetic Gaussian noise injection ranging from $-20\text{ dB}$ to $+20\text{ dB}$ SNR to benchmark detection degradation under severe RF jamming and adverse weather attenuation:

| Input SNR (dB) | CA-CFAR $\text{mAP}_{50}$ | YOLOv3 $\text{mAP}_{50}$ | YOLOv9-C (S-128) $\text{mAP}_{50}$ | Proposed Adaptive $\text{mAP}_{50}$ |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **$-20\text{ dB}$** | 0.04 | 0.12 | 0.22 | 0.28 |
| **$-15\text{ dB}$** | 0.08 | 0.21 | 0.35 | 0.41 |
| **$-10\text{ dB}$** | 0.14 | 0.32 | 0.48 | 0.54 |
| **$-5\text{ dB}$**  | 0.22 | 0.41 | 0.54 | 0.60 |
| **$0\text{ dB}$**   | 0.28 | 0.46 | 0.58 | 0.64 |
| **$+10\text{ dB}$** | 0.31 | 0.48 | 0.60 | 0.66 |
| **$+20\text{ dB}$** | 0.32 | 0.49 | 0.60 | 0.67 |

### Key Takeaways
1. **Classical CFAR Collapse Below $0\text{ dB}$**: CFAR detection rate drops below $15\%$ once SNR crosses $-10\text{ dB}$ due to noise power overestimation.
2. **Deep Learning Robustness**: The deep model maintains $>45\%\text{ mAP}$ even at $-10\text{ dB}$, confirming the network learns coherent spatial phase structures rather than raw amplitude thresholds.
