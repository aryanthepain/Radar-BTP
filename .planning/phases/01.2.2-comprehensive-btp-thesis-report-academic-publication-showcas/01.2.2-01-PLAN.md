<action>
Draft the in-depth comparative literature synthesis examining the 5 key prior art bodies:
1. **RadDet (Zi Huang et al., IEEE ICASSP 2025)**:
   - Real-world 40,000-frame 500 MHz wideband radar dataset.
   - Max-hold spectrogram transformation across 3 resolutions ($S-128^2, M-256^2, L-512^2$).
   - Performance evaluation across 11 classes, 6 SNR tiers ($-20\text{ dB}$ to $+20\text{ dB}$), and 4 detectors (YOLOv3, YOLOv6-M, YOLOv9-M, RT-DETR-L).
   - Core limitation: Static fixed-resolution inference where all frames are processed at $512^2$ ($2.23\text{ ms}$, high FLOPs) or $128^2$ ($1.12\text{ ms}$, lower mAP on dense frames), lacking dynamic runtime resolution scaling.
2. **RAVEN (CVPR 2026)**:
   - High-resolution radar tensor learning with spatial-temporal attention.
   - Trade-off between multi-modal fusion and standalone RF detection.
   - Core limitation: Heavy computational footprint ($>150\text{ GFLOPs}$) unsuitable for edge automotive microcontrollers.
3. **AdaRadar (CVPR 2026)**:
   - Adaptive chirp rate streaming and sensor-level modulation.
   - Core limitation: Requires specialized, software-defined transmitter hardware capable of dynamic agile chirp sweeps; does not address adaptive computation on pre-digitized or standardized automotive radar streams.
4. **Alexander Karlsson's PhD Thesis (KTH / Saab Surveillance 2023)**:
   - Industrial 77 GHz radar baseline (Saab SIRS 1600: $B=160\text{ MHz}$, $T_c=58.8\ \mu\text{s}$, $1^\circ$ azimuth beamwidth, Taylor $S=-40\text{ dB}$).
   - 4 major contributions: Stepped-frequency non-coherent pulse compression (1D CNN + MLP), Data-driven Track-Before-Detect (2-stage CNN on $64 \times 32$ RT maps achieving $P_d>90\%$ at $3\text{ dB}$ SNR), Drone vs bird micro-Doppler classification with synthetic cylinder modeling and latent $k=4$ autoencoder QDA ($96.4\%$ accuracy), and Low-angle naval tracking under sea multipath with complex monopulse ratio and Dual Kalman filtering.
5. **Dr. Tapas Kumar Pal's Survey (DRDO, IEEE SPACE 2025)**:
   - Defense radar systems perspective: Comprehensive radar range equation with system losses ($P_r = \frac{P_t G_t G_r \lambda^2 \sigma}{(4\pi)^3 R^4 L}$), GAN-based clutter generation, RL adaptive beamforming with DQN ($Q(s, a) \approx f(s, a; \theta)$), integrated state estimation with Kalman filtering, 8-bit integer quantization ($w_q = \text{round}(w \cdot 2^n)/2^n$), and gradient saliency ($\partial f(x)/\partial x$) for explainability.
</action>

<action>
Formulate the structured Research-Gap Matrix and deep mathematical specifications for the proposed thesis direction:
1. **7-Axis Research-Gap Matrix**:
   - Contrast existing radar detection literature along 7 critical axes:
     1. *Input Resolution*: Fixed ($128^2$ or $512^2$) vs. Dynamic input-adaptive resolution.
     2. *Compute Allocation*: Static uniform FLOPs vs. Clutter/SNR-conditioned compute.
     3. *Data Representation*: Post-CFAR sparse point cloud vs. Pre-CFAR 2.5D Range-Azimuth-Doppler spectrum.
     4. *Inference Topology*: Monolithic feed-forward vs. Dynamic layer early-exit.
     5. *RF Pretraining*: None (random init / ImageNet weights) vs. Self-supervised masked RF spectrogram modeling.
     6. *Optimization Objective*: Single-metric mAP maximization vs. Multi-objective constrained Pareto optimization.
     7. *Edge Feasibility*: Non-deterministic super-resolution ($O(M^3)$ MUSIC) vs. Sub-millisecond deterministic neural execution ($<2\text{ ms}$).
2. **5 Candidate Adaptive Computation Methodologies (D-03)**:
   - *Method 1: Cascaded Multi-Resolution Triage*: Lightweight $128^2$ fast-path detector handling $\approx 80\%$ of clean/high-SNR frames ($1.12\text{ ms}$ latency); ambiguous frames (prediction confidence $< \gamma_{th}$ or entropy $> H_{th}$) escalated to $256^2$ or $512^2$ deep detector.
   - *Method 2: Cross-Resolution Knowledge Distillation*: High-resolution $512^2$ teacher model transferring multi-scale spatial-frequency representations into an ultra-compact $128^2$ student model via feature imitation loss ($\mathcal{L}_{KD} = \|\mathbf{F}_{\text{teacher}} - \phi(\mathbf{F}_{\text{student}})\|_2^2$).
   - *Method 3: Dynamic Layer Early-Exit & Conditional Computation*: Multi-head early exits embedded across intermediate GELAN/PGI backbone stages. Easy scenes exit at stage 2; complex multi-target scenes proceed to stage 4.
   - *Method 4: Self-Supervised RF Pretraining & Representation Learning*: Masked autoencoding on raw Range-Doppler spectrogram patches to learn fundamental RF propagation primitives without manual bounding box labels.
   - *Method 5: Reinforcement Learning / Learned Policy Allocation*: Formulating resource allocation as a Markov Decision Process (MDP) where an agent $\pi(a|s)$ observes frame SNR/clutter statistics and selects the optimal compute budget.
3. **Formal Mathematical Formulations (D-04)**:
   - Primary Constrained Optimization:
     $$\min_{\theta, \pi} \mathbb{E}_{x \sim \mathcal{D}} [C(x, \pi(x))] \quad \text{s.t.} \quad \text{mAP}(f(x, \pi(x))) \ge \text{mAP}_{\text{oracle}} - \epsilon \quad \text{and} \quad \text{Latency}_{P95} \le \tau_{\max}$$
   - Multi-Objective Pareto Loss:
     $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{total}} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{det}}(\hat{y}, y) + \lambda \frac{C(x)}{C_{\max}} + \gamma \mathcal{U}(x)$$
</action>
